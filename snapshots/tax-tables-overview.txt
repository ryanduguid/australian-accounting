About tax tables

We produce a range of tax tables to help you work out how much to withhold from payments you make to your employees or other payees. A tax withheld calculator that calculates the correct amount of tax to withhold is available.

Tax tables with an asterisk (‘*’) have downloadable look-up tables available in portable document format (PDF). To get a copy of the PDF, select the tax table you need and go to the heading 'Using this table'.

Tax tables for previous years are also available.

Important Information – July 2026 updates

The tax cuts made by the Treasury Laws Amendment (More Cost of Living Relief) Act 2025 (the Act) will apply to the 2026–27 income year resulting in updates to all 15 withholding schedules and 12 tax tables from 1 July 2026.

Specifically, the Act amends the Income Tax Rates Act 1986, which modified income tax rates for individuals for the 2026–27 income year. The Act also amends the Medicare Levy Act 1986 and the A New Tax System (Medicare Levy Surcharge – Fringe Benefits) Act 1999 which increased Medicare levy low-income thresholds.

Additionally, updates have been made due to annual indexing of the repayment income thresholds for study and training support loans and other rates and thresholds.

Finally, minor changes for formatting, style and clarity of expression have been made where relevant. These updates also include up-to-date examples, cap or payment limit amounts.
