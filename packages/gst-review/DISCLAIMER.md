Ryan Duguid is not a registered tax agent or BAS agent. Project support is limited to software issues reproduced with fabricated data. Do not send taxpayer information or request advice, return preparation, tax-treatment confirmation, or lodgement.

gst-review gives coded bank lines a review state and compares lodged BAS figures
with the ledger lines it is handed. It does not provide professional advice. It is
not a BAS, a revised BAS, a lodgement, an audit opinion or a determination under
the GST Act.

Outputs can be wrong, incomplete, stale, or unsuitable for a given set of facts.
The review takes registration dates, GST codes, supplier status, periods, lodged
figures and the accounting basis as the caller supplies them and does not verify
them. Legislation is recompiled and ATO instructions change. Confirm the operative
GST Act compilation at legislation.gov.au and the current BAS instructions at
ato.gov.au, and check the facts against source records before acting. Leave
corrections, revisions and lodgement with a registered practitioner.

The engine does not decide whether an entity was required to be registered,
whether a supply is taxable, GST-free or input taxed on its merits, what a
transfer between entities is, whether a suggested coding is right, which
accounting basis applies, or whether a difference needs a revised BAS. States
and differences on those points are review prompts, not conclusions.

client-identifying ABNs, bank details, identity documents, client files, or other
sensitive personal information in issues, pull requests, examples, tests, or
repository content. Fixtures in this repository are fabricated.
