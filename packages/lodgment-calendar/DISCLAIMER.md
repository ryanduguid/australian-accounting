lodgment-calendar lists the due dates the ATO publishes for the 2026–27 lodgment program
against the facts it is handed. It does not provide professional advice. It is not a
lodgement, an ATO client account, a deferral request or a determination of when any
obligation is due.

Outputs can be wrong, incomplete, stale, or unsuitable for a given set of facts. The
calendar takes entity type, lodgement history, income and liability thresholds,
taxable status and lodgement method as the caller supplies them and does not verify
them. The ATO says its program list is a guide only, can set an earlier date for a
client, and changes its pages during the year. Confirm each client's dates in Online
services for agents, the practitioner lodgment service or on the statement itself
before acting.

`UNKNOWN` rows are the result, not a gap to fill. Do not replace one with its nominal
date or a guess.
